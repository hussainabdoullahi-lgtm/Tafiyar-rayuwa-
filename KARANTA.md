# Tafiyar Rayuwa - gina APK a GitHub (kyauta, ba watermark)

1. Buɗe asusun GitHub (github.com/signup), tabbatar da imel.
2. Ƙirƙiri sabon repo: "New repository" > suna: tafiyar-rayuwa > Public > Create.
3. Cire zip ɗin ka loda DUKKAN abin da ke ciki (har da babban fayil .github) zuwa repo.
4. Buɗe tab "Actions" > "Build APK" > "Run workflow".
5. Jira minti 5-8 har ya yi kore (nasara), buɗe aikin, ka sauke "Tafiyar-Rayuwa-APK" daga "Artifacts".
6. Cire zip ɗin da ka sauke, shigar da app-debug.apk a waya (kunna "Install unknown apps").
